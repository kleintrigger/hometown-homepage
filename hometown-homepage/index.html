Visit Malabon

Meet the animals at Malabon Zoo

Get up close with a variety of animals while exploring the aquarium and botanical gardens at Malabon Zoo.

Discover Malabon's history

Step back in time by visiting historic churches, ancestral houses, and landmarks that tell the story of Malabon's rich heritage.

Taste the flavors of Malabon

Treat yourself to local favorites like Pancit Malabon and traditional Filipino kakanin.
